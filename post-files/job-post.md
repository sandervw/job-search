### Description

**Company Overview**

SundaySky is a category-defining SaaS platform that enables enterprises and upper mid-market organizations to deliver highly personalized video experiences at scale across the entire customer lifecycle. Built for modern, data-driven teams, the platform empowers marketers, CX leaders and field organizations to transform complex data into dynamic, relevant video communications that deepen engagement, improve the customer experience, increase conversion, and strengthen long-term relationships. Founded in Tel Aviv in 2007, SundaySky serves some of the world’s most recognized banking, financial services, and insurance firms. As the pioneer of AI-powered video personalization, SundaySky reimagines how enterprises communicate. Moving beyond static, one-size-fits-all content to intelligent, adaptive video experiences. The platform combines generative AI, real-time rendering, and an intuitive creation workflow to make personalized video fast, scalable, and measurable. By unifying creation, personalization, distribution, and optimization in a single system, SundaySky enables teams to deliver emotionally resonant experiences that drive outcomes, turning video into a core growth and engagement engine rather than a costly, slow-moving production exercise.

  

**Investment History**

In July 2022, SundaySky completed a $100 million investment from the private equity firm Clearhaven Partners, providing capital for growth and innovation. This investment completes the transition of the business from a managed services company to a SaaS company, enabling the next stage of growth. Clearhaven is a Boston-based private equity firm focused exclusively on software and technology investments. Founded in 2019 by an investor-operator team with over 50 years of combined experience, Clearhaven was purpose-built to partner with differentiated, growth-stage software companies. The firm invests thematically in high-quality B2B software businesses, supporting management teams seeking an operationally focused partner to drive transformational growth.

**About the Role**

We are seeking a Senior Full-Stack Engineer who can independently design, build, test, and operate complex capabilities across our web application, backend services, data integrations, and video creation platform. This is a hands-on individual-contributor role for an engineer who can take ownership of significant product capabilities from technical discovery through production deployment. The ideal candidate combines strong frontend and backend expertise with sound architectural judgment, product thinking, and the ability to raise the technical performance of the broader team. This person should be able to work from product requirements and design concepts, identify technical gaps and edge cases, propose an implementation approach, and deliver production-quality software with limited oversight.
