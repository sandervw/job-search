# Companies of Interest

## Publishing & Literature (especially weird/dark fantasy/specialty)

- **Tor Books** (Macmillan imprint, the big fantasy/SF publisher)
- **Subterranean Press** - specialty press right in your wheelhouse (Vance, CAS, Hodgson reprints)
- **Tartarus Press** - UK-based weird fiction publisher
- **Valancourt Books** - gothic/weird fiction revival press
- **Folio Society** - you already collect their stuff
- **Easton Press** (MBI, Inc.)
- **Chaosium** - publishes Lovecraftian/weird fiction alongside their RPGs
- **Penguin Random House** - massive, but owns a lot of the fantasy/literary imprints
- **Dover Publications** - reprints classic/obscure texts
- **Night Shade Books** (Skyhorse) - dark fantasy/SF focus
- **Titan Books** - genre fiction, art books, film tie-ins
- **Project Gutenberg**

## Audiobooks & Audio Production

- **Audible** (Amazon) - the giant
- **Libro.fm** - indie-friendly audiobook platform
- **Blackstone Publishing** - major independent audiobook producer
- **Tantor Media** - big audiobook producer, lots of genre fiction
- **Recorded Books** (RBmedia) - another large audiobook house
- **Graphic Audio** - they do full-cast dramatized audiobooks, very atmospheric
- **Librivox**

## Music & Music Services

- **Bandcamp** (Songtradr now owns it, but still)
- **Qobuz** - high-fidelity music streaming/purchasing, classical strength
- **Tidal** - lossless streaming
- **Presto Music** - you already use them, UK-based classical retailer
- **Spotify** - massive, obviously
- **DistroKid** or **CDBaby** - music distribution (relevant to the dungeon synth scene)

## Gaming (dark fantasy / ARPG / atmospheric)

- **FromSoftware** - the gold standard, but Japan-based (Bandai Namco handles Western ops)
- **CD Projekt Red** - The Witcher, dark fantasy through and through
- **Bethesda** (ZeniMax/Microsoft) - Morrowind's home
- **Supergiant Games** - Hades, atmospheric and well-crafted
- **Free League Publishing** - Swedish TTRPG publisher (Mork Borg, Forbidden Lands - dark, atmospheric)
- **Devolver Digital** - indie publisher with great taste in atmospheric/weird games
- **11 bit studios** - Frostpunk, dark atmospheric games

## Bonsai / Horticulture

- **Brussel's Bonsai** - largest US bonsai nursery/retailer
- **Bonsai Mirai** (Ryan Neil's operation) - premium education/community
- **Monrovia** - major wholesale nursery

## Running / Fitness Brands

- **Brooks Running**
- **Salomon**
- **Hoka** (Deckers Brands)
- **Strava** - the social/data side of running

## Food (low-FODMAP adjacent)

- **FODY Foods** - literally a low-FODMAP specialty brand
- **Serious Eats** (Dotdash Meredith) - if you ever wanted editorial/data work adjacent to good food writing
