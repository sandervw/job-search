# Leap: FinTech Lead

## Dealbreakers
- Fully remote? Any travel?
- Direct reports on the org chart?
- Who owns reviews, PIPs, raises?
- Is there an Engineering manager?
- IC track (Staff/Principal) that stays IC?

## Comp
- Bonus? Equity or profit units? Health premiums, 401(k) match?

## Stability
- Profitable, or Nexa-funded?
- Nexa's 2-3 year plan: sale, add-ons?
- Layoffs in the last 2 years?
- Why is this role open?

## Daily Life
- Eastern hours in practice? India standup times?
- On-call rotation and page frequency?
- Weekly hours in meetings/1:1s?
- What drives deadlines?

## Team
- Who is the partner team? Quality?
- Budget for AI seats and agent tooling?

## Prep
- Rehearse live demo: parallel agents, worktrees, skills, MCP.
- Stories: vendor-collapse rebuild and documentation; OFAC/audit trails; Claude Code rollout/security; Paymentus integration.
- Review: idempotency keys, integer cents, webhooks/retries, state machines, strangler fig.
- Skim TILA/Reg Z, ECOA, FCRA basics.
- Pitch line: "Builder who leads through design, review, mentoring; not a people manager."
- Let them name salary first.
